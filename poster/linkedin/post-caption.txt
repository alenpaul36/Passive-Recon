2026's AI breaches weren't model failures. They were old mistakes with an agent in the middle.

Over-trusted input, over-scoped secrets, unvetted dependencies. Agents made each one reachable with a sentence.

I mapped five of this year's shifts to OWASP (LLM and Agentic Top 10), Google SAIF, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and MITRE ATLAS, with a test you can run for each:

Two things stood out while mapping:
1. Prompt injection has seven names across the frameworks. OWASP, SAIF and ATLAS tell you the fix; NIST, ISO and the EU AI Act ask you to prove you tested for it.
2. Neither ISO/IEC 42001 nor the NIST AI RMF names least privilege for agents. That control still comes from ISO/IEC 27001 and NIST SP 800-53.
